Who we are

Market IQ Agencies is a specialist commercial consulting firm founded by Lawson
Govender a results-driven leader with over 30 years experience within the  pharma and
FMCG markets in South Africa.
We combine in-depth market knowledge with innovative marketing strategies, we help
businesses strengthen their brands, expand their market reach, and achieve sustainable
growth.
Our approach is built on understanding each client's unique needs, identifying
opportunities, and delivering tailored solutions that turn business objectives into
measurable results and growth, optimise market access, and build high-performance
sales teams.

Founder — Lawson Govender

National Sales Management

Leading national sales teams with clear strategies, practical targets and disciplined execution. Expertise in
territory planning, sales forecasting, performance management and coaching to drive sustainable growth.

National Key Account Management

Building strong commercial relationships with pharmacy groups, wholesalers, distributors and retail partners.
Focused on account planning, negotiations, product listings and expanding market access.

Market Insights and Marketing Strategy

Turning market knowledge, customer insights and sales data into practical commercial plans. Expertise in
identifying growth opportunities, product positioning, launch planning and aligning marketing activity with

Sales Force Effectiveness and Coaching

planning and clear performance measures. Helping representatives strengthen customer engagement and
deliver results.
